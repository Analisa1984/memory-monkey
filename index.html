Welcome to Memory Monkey

About Us

Memory Monkey is designed for anyone searching to have some fun during their leisure time. Memory Monkey is a memory game and is relatively easy to play and games such as these may improve memory.

This game has a total of 10 rounds and each round gets progressively harder. The game is designed to test and improve your memory skills. In each round, you will be shown a series of images that you need to remember in the correct order. The number of images increases with each round. The game keeps track of your score seen in the red oval image. The objective of the game is to get the highest score possible by entering the images in the correct order as shown to you. A maximum score of 100 points can be achieved if you enter all the images correctly in all 10 rounds. The game also allows you to delete your last entry if you make a mistake. At the end of the game you will be given the option to start a new game. Enjoy and have fun!

  • Round 1 --> 1 image to remember
  • Round 2 --> 2 images to remember
  • Round 3 --> 3 images to remember
  • Round 4 --> 4 images to remember
  • Round 5 --> 5 images to remember
  • Round 6 --> 6 images to remember
  • Round 7 --> 7 images to remember
  • Round 8 --> 8 images to remember
  • Round 9 --> 9 images to remember
  • Round 10 --> 10 images to remember
How to Play

To play Memory Monkey, click on the "Play Now" button below. You will be taken to the game page where you can start playing. Each round will display a series of images that you need to remember in the correct order. After the images are shown, use the buttons provided to enter the images in the order you remember them. You can use the "Delete" button to remove your last entry if needed. Once you have entered all the images for the round, click on the "Submit" button to see if you got them right. Your score will be updated based on your performance. Continue playing through all 10 rounds to achieve the highest score possible!

Enjoy and have fun!
