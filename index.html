Welcome to Memory Monkey

About Us

Memory Monkey is designed for anyone searching to have some fun during their leisure time. Memory Monkey is a memory game and is relatively easy to play and games such as these may improve memory.

How to Play

Welcome to Memory Monkey! To play this cheeky mind game, first a series of images for a few seconds. Your task will be to remember the images and their order. After the images disappear, you will be prompted to select the images in the correct order. The game will keep track of your score and rounds. Try to get the highest score possible!

If you enter the sequence incorrectly, you will lose and the game then ends. If you enter the sequence of items correctly, you will advance to the next round.

Each round will have an additional item to the sequence. SO, the first round will show you one item, the second round will show you two items, the third round will show you three items, and so on.

Enjoy and have fun!
